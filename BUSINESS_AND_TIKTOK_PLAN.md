### Exact Shipping Supplies Checklist (To Go With Your UPS 4x6 Thermal Printer)
1. **UPS 4x6 Thermal Printer (You Already Have This! ✅)**
   - Set printer paper size to **`4" x 6" (100mm x 150mm)`**.
   - In **TikTok Seller Center -> Orders -> Shipping Settings**, change label format from `8.5x11 PDF` to **`4x6 Thermal Label`** so labels print in 1 second with zero cutting or taping.
2. **4x6 Direct Thermal Fanfold Labels**
   - Free from UPS for UPS shipments, or grab a stack of 1,000 fanfold 4x6 thermal labels (~$14 on Amazon).
3. **Self-Sealing Poly Mailers (Use for 80% of Your Catalog — Plushies, Backpacks, Puppets, LED Crowns)**
   - **Rule:** Never ship soft plushies, backpacks, or puppets in cardboard boxes! Boxes add 8–10 oz of extra weight (pushing you out of the cheap sub-1-lb USPS Ground Advantage tier) and waste time taping.
   - **Size 1 (Small/Medium): `10" x 13"` Poly Mailers** — Fits 1–2 Pokémon plushies, Boba plush, Boxy Boo, Rainbow Friends, or LED Flower Crowns. Total weight stays **8 oz – 12 oz** (cheapest shipping tier!).
   - **Size 2 (Large): `14.5" x 19"` Poly Mailers** — Fits Sanrio Plush Backpacks, 24" Jeffy Puppets, Snorlax, or 3–4 plush bundles. Still stays **under 1 lb (15.9 oz)**!
   - *Branding Tip:* Buy **Neon Pink or Electric Cyan Poly Mailers** so packages stand out on doorsteps and in customer unboxing videos.
4. **Bubble Mailers & Small Boxes (Use for 20% of Catalog — Electronics & Rigid Toys)**
   - **`8.5" x 12"` Padded Bubble Mailers** for Neon LED Masks and small light-up toys.
   - **`12" x 9" x 6"` Corrugated Boxes** (or wrap the toy box in 1 layer of bubble wrap inside a `14.5" x 19"` poly mailer) for the Interactive Voice Robot, Microscope Case, and Pixel Swords.
5. **Digital Shipping Scale ($15–$18)**
   - Weigh each toy once in ounces. *(Pro Tip: Many Happy Toys photos already list the gram weight like `79g`, `105g`, `218g`, `260g`—divide grams by `28.35` to get ounces, plus `0.5 oz` for a poly mailer!)*
6. **The "USPS SCAN Sheet" Trick (Crucial for TikTok Shop Health!)**
   - At the end of your Mon/Wed/Fri packing session, print a **USPS SCAN Form (End-of-Day Manifest)** on your 4x6 thermal printer.
   - When you drop the bags at the post office (or hand them to your mail carrier via Free USPS Porch Pickup), they scan **that single barcode** and all 20–50 packages immediately mark as **"Scanned & In Transit"** on TikTok Shop at once!
