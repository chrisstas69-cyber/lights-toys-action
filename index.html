About Lights Toys Action

Our Story

Founded in New York by Chris Anastasi, Lights Toys Action (and our event division, Tasty Treats NYC) brings over 20 years of hands-on experience delighting families across Long Island and NYC street fairs, fireworks spectaculars, and carnivals.

Through our direct partnership with Happy Toys in Maspeth, NY—the largest toy distributor in New York—we hand-pick toys that light up, move, and spark real excitement, bringing carnival-level joy straight to your doorstep via our website and TikTok Shop.
